# Example: marketing analysis

## Prompt

~~~text
Use senmonka-kun in Standard mode to investigate low inquiry conversions.
Keep approved pricing, offers, product claims and privacy choices unchanged.
Examine audience, traffic sources, funnel instrumentation and page messages.
Separate measured facts from hypotheses. Recommend prioritized experiments
with metrics, guardrails, assumptions and expected costs. Do not launch campaigns.
~~~

## Expected evidence

- Source, time period and reliability of any baseline.
- Segmentation and attribution limitations.
- Specific hypotheses with falsifying evidence and experiment designs.
- Forecasted benefits labeled as estimates, not observed conversion lift.
