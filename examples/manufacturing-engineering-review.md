# Example: production process investigation

## Prompt

~~~text
Use senmonka-kun in Deep mode to examine rising rework on a machining line.
Keep approved inspection limits and procedures unchanged. Examine scrap by
lot, shift and step; measurement reliability, process flow and other causes.
Design a safe, authorized pilot with measurable acceptance criteria.
Do not change live machine settings, tooling or product-release rules.
~~~

## Expected evidence

- Process map, data definitions, source records and measured baseline.
- Quality/cycle-time analysis only when data support the method.
- Candidate causes separated from evidence; no claimed savings without a trial.
- Trial safeguards, verification and engineering/quality approval gates.
