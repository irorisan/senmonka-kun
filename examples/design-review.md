# Example: design review

## Prompt

~~~text
Use senmonka-kun in Standard mode to critique the product page's visual design.
Preserve the approved brand colors, component system, and content decisions.
Compare mobile and desktop screenshots, inspect hierarchy, type, spacing and
accessibility, and provide two evidence-grounded improvements.
Treat a brand redesign as a separate proposal requiring approval.
~~~

## Expected evidence

- Actual screen sizes/states and current design constraints.
- Specific observations, not just 'looks professional'.
- Impact and trade-offs of each option.
- Render/accessibility tests proposed or performed, clearly distinguished.
