# Example: mechanical design review

## Prompt

~~~text
Use senmonka-kun in Deep mode to review this conveyor drive assembly.
Preserve the approved duty cycle and layout. Examine loads, shaft and bearing
failure modes, units, tolerance stack-ups, alternative explanations and
required independent tests. Drawings and one datasheet are available but
no physical test reports. Do not modify the design or authorize operation.
~~~

## Expected evidence

- Source revisions, load assumptions and missing inputs.
- Traceable calculations or hypotheses, not fabricated numerical ratings.
- Checks for credible failure modes, fit, serviceability and safety.
- An approval-gated verification plan with qualified physical review.
