# Marketing methods

Use for messaging, audience analysis, campaign planning, acquisition/conversion, and channel strategy.

## Primary material

Verified product capabilities and pricing, customer research, channel attribution and traffic, consented analytics, funnel events, actual campaign costs, conversion baselines, retention cohorts, and public competitor evidence with dates.

## Practical methods

1. **Audience and job-to-be-done:** identify whose problem the product solves, purchase constraints and competing alternatives; segment only when supported by evidence.
2. **Positioning:** tie claims to documented benefits and differentiators; prohibit invented testimonials, unsupported superlatives or misleading urgency.
3. **Funnel diagnosis:** identify exposure, engagement, qualified action and conversion; evaluate instrumentation quality, sample size and attribution limits.
4. **Experiment design:** specify hypothesis, primary metric, guardrail metrics, control, duration/volume needs and stopping criteria *before* running a test.
5. **Economics:** estimate CAC, margin, retention and payback with assumptions visible; distinguish forecasts from measured ROI.
6. **Channel and ethics:** consider privacy, consent, accessibility, trust, fatigue, brand consistency and dark-pattern risks.

Do not promise lift without a test. In small samples, prefer honest uncertainty and directional findings. Do not change approved pricing, policies, offers or brand claims without authorization.
